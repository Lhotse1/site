Dashboard

Coffee shop sales dashboard

RolePersonal project
IndustryRetail / hospitality (sample data)
ToolsExcel · PivotTables · Slicers

The problem

Raw transaction-level sales data for a small coffee shop chain isn't easy to read at a glance. To spot trends in revenue, busy trading times and best-selling products, someone would otherwise need to build separate summaries by hand for every store and time period.

What I did

I built a single-page Excel dashboard driven by PivotTables and PivotCharts, with a slicer to filter every view by store location (Covent Garden, King's Cross and Moorgate). The dashboard brings together:

  • Revenue by month, to track the overall trend
  • Transactions by day of week and by hour, to show when each store is busiest
  • Top 15 products by quantity sold and revenue
  • A breakdown of transactions by product category (coffee, tea, bakery, etc.)

The result

A single filterable view that lets anyone, without touching a formula, switch between stores and instantly see revenue trends, peak trading hours and which products are driving sales. The result is a simple, effective dashboard that helps store managers make more informed, data-driven decisions.
